Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 20, 2026

This policy explains what personal information Tamara Wilder ("we", "us") collects through this website, how it is used, and the choices you have. We keep it short and plain on purpose.

Information we collect

  • Book orders. When you use the order form, we collect your name, email address, shipping address, quantity, and any notes you choose to add.
  • Messages. When you contact us through the contact form or by email, we collect your name, email address, and the message you send.
  • Email list. If you join the email list, we collect your email address.
  • Basic technical data. Like most websites, our hosting provider may automatically record standard server information such as your IP address, browser type, and the pages requested, for security and reliability.

This site does not use advertising cookies or third-party analytics or tracking tools.

How we use your information

  • To follow up about your order, including payment and shipping.
  • To reply to your messages.
  • To send occasional updates about new books and news, if you joined the email list. Every email will let you unsubscribe.
  • To keep the site secure and working.

Who we share it with

We do not sell your personal information. We share it only with service providers who help us run the site, and only as needed for that purpose. These include the form service that delivers order and contact submissions to us (Formspree), our website hosting provider, and any email service used for the email list. We may also disclose information if the law requires it.

Links to other sites, such as Amazon, lead to services with their own privacy practices. This policy does not cover them.

How long we keep it

We keep order and message information only as long as needed to complete your order, answer you, and meet any legal or record-keeping duties. We keep email list addresses until you unsubscribe or ask us to remove you.

Your choices

You can ask us to tell you what personal information we hold about you, correct it, or delete it, and you can unsubscribe from emails at any time. Depending on where you live, you may have additional rights under local law. To make a request, email us at the address below.

Security

We take reasonable steps to protect your information, including the use of encrypted (HTTPS) connections. No method of transmission or storage is completely secure, so we cannot guarantee absolute security.

Children

This site is not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from them. If you believe a child has sent us information, contact us and we will delete it.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. The "last updated" date above shows when it last changed.
